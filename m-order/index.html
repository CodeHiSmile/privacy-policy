At M-Order, protecting the privacy of our users is one of our priorities. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected and recorded by M-Order and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or need more information about this policy, please contact us using the support channel described below.

01

Information We Collect and How We Use It

The information M-Order processes depends on the features you use and the permissions you choose to grant. It may include:

  • Information you provide, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and optional date of birth, gender, and phone number. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the account information you authorize that provider to share.
  • Store and business information, such as store details, staff invitations and roles, product and topping names, prices and photos, orders, amounts received, expenses, and notes you enter.
  • App and device information, such as device type, operating system, app version, language, and diagnostic data.
  • Notification information, such as device registration tokens processed by Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver app notifications.

We may use this information to provide and maintain M-Order, personalize your store experience, synchronize records, calculate business reports, manage staff access, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors, communicate service-related updates, and comply with legal obligations.

02

Log Files

M-Order follows a standard procedure of using log files. When you use the app, these files may record information such as Internet Protocol (IP) address, device and browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring or exit pages, app events, and diagnostic details.

This technical information is used to analyze trends, administer and secure the app, understand feature usage, and improve reliability. We do not use log files to directly identify you unless needed for security, support, or legal compliance.

03

Service Providers

M-Order uses service providers to operate account sign-in, cloud data storage, image uploads, and notifications. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. You can review their privacy information below.

  • Google Firebase: authentication, storage of account and store records in Cloud Firestore, and push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Firebase privacy information ↗
  • Google and Apple: optional account sign-in. Google Privacy Policy ↗ and Apple Privacy Policy ↗
  • Cloudflare R2: storage and delivery of images uploaded through M-Order's file service. Cloudflare Privacy Policy ↗
04

How Information Is Shared

Your profile information is visible to managers of the stores you join. Store records are available to authorized members according to their roles and permissions so they can manage orders, products, expenses, and staff.

We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above to operate M-Order, or disclosed when necessary to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, or protect users and the service.

05

Your Choices and Account Deletion

You can review and edit your profile, clear optional personal information, manage linked sign-in accounts, or delete your account in the app under Khác → Cài đặt tài khoản (More → Account settings). Account deletion requires confirmation and may require you to sign in again.

Deleting your account removes your profile and store memberships. If you are the only manager of a store, its data is also deleted as described in the app's confirmation. Records belonging to a store with other managers may remain available to that store.

You choose which photos to upload. You can manage photo, camera, and notification permissions in your device settings. Disabling a permission may limit the related feature.

06

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide M-Order, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal requirements.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

07

Children's Information

Protecting children while they use online services is important to us. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, monitor, and guide their children's online activity.

M-Order does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided this kind of information through M-Order, please contact us promptly. We will make reasonable efforts to remove it from our records.

08

Online Privacy Policy Only

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through M-Order and its related online services. It does not apply to information collected offline or through channels outside the app unless this policy is expressly referenced there.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect information.

10
